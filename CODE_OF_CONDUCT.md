# Code of conduct

Cavalry is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone who participates, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of contribution.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Give actionable feedback and accept corrections in good faith.
- Respect privacy. Do not post another person's private information or any real financial data.
- Make room for people with different experience levels and communication styles.
- Stop behavior that a maintainer identifies as disruptive or unsafe.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, impersonation, doxxing, and publishing private communications without permission are not acceptable. Spam, malicious contributions, and attempts to obtain credentials or user data are also prohibited.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, release channels, and other spaces where someone is representing the Cavalry project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For conduct visible on GitHub, use the content menu to report or block the account and, when safe, flag the item for a repository maintainer. Sensitive conduct reports should not be posted in a public issue. If no private maintainer contact is available, use [GitHub's abuse-reporting channel](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).

Security vulnerabilities and accidental disclosure of credentials or financial data must instead follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently exclude participants whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. Reports will be handled as privately as the available channel permits. Retaliation against someone who makes a good-faith report is prohibited.
