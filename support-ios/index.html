Quick guide

Start here.

01

Choose your workbook

In Settings → Workbook, open a workbook saved on your device or create a new one. Check the workbook name before adding transactions.

02

Save an independent copy

Open Settings → Files & Data → Export Copy, then choose Save to Files or another destination in the iOS share sheet. Your exported HTML file contains your financial information.

03

Open an exported workbook

Choose Settings → Files & Data → Import Workbook, select your file, and review its details before choosing Import & open.

04

Check your iCloud connection

Open Cloud to review the current account and workbook status. iOS uses the Apple Account signed into iCloud in device Settings. Workbooks stay in Cavalry’s private iCloud library, rather than appearing as ordinary iCloud Drive files.

Your local workbook remains available when offline. If you see a sync error, keep your local copy and record the message before reporting the issue.

05

Review your notes

Write a note on Home, then choose Review transactions. Cavalry prepares possible entries on your device. Check each amount, account, category, and date before recording it in your workbook.

06

Manage your saved information

Settings → Workbook → Recovery copies helps you review earlier local versions. Clear note removes the current note and prepared entries.

Read the retention and deletion controls for local, iCloud, exported, and Keychain information.

Help us understand the problem.

When opening an issue, include your device model, iOS version, Cavalry version, what you were trying to do, and the steps that led to the problem. Describe what happened and what you expected. Use made-up amounts and names in examples.
